# PSP Internal Landscape Video Script

## Purpose
A short internal leadership video for WhatsApp/Telegram circulation to the PSP CEC.

## Runtime
35–45 seconds

## Tone
Serious, disciplined, urgent, credible.

## Voiceover / On-screen Script

### Scene 1 (0:00–0:05)
**On-screen:**
PSP is not collapsing.
But it may be fading.

**Voiceover:**
PSP is not collapsing. But it may be fading.

---

### Scene 2 (0:05–0:11)
**On-screen:**
The committed core
Still loyal. Still holding the line.

**Voiceover:**
The committed core is still loyal, still holding the line.

---

### Scene 3 (0:11–0:18)
**On-screen:**
The demoralized middle
Still here. Less sure. Waiting for direction.

**Voiceover:**
The demoralized middle is still here, but less sure and waiting for direction.

---

### Scene 4 (0:18–0:25)
**On-screen:**
The drifting fringe
Not angry. Just slowly disappearing.

**Voiceover:**
The drifting fringe is not angry. It is simply disappearing.

---

### Scene 5 (0:25–0:32)
**On-screen:**
The younger reformists
Energetic. Future-facing. Impatient for renewal.

**Voiceover:**
The younger reformists are energetic, future-facing, and impatient for renewal.

---

### Scene 6 (0:32–0:41)
**On-screen:**
Loyalty is outrunning confidence.
If we do not act,
drift becomes decline.

**Voiceover:**
Loyalty is outrunning confidence. If we do not act, drift becomes decline.

---

## Closing message option
For internal discussion only.

## Notes
- Keep typography large and readable on a phone.
- Assume many viewers will watch muted, so on-screen text must stand alone.
- Background score, if used, should be restrained and serious, not cinematic propaganda.
