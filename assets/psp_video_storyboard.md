# PSP Internal Landscape Storyboard

## Format
- Platform: WhatsApp / Telegram
- Aspect ratio: 9:16 preferred, 4:5 acceptable fallback
- Duration: 35–45 seconds
- Style: PSP-branded, clean, internal leadership brief

## Scene Plan

### Scene 1 — Opening warning
- Visual: dark background, subtle red geometric motif
- Text:
  - PSP is not collapsing.
  - But it may be fading.
- Purpose: immediate tension without melodrama

### Scene 2 — The committed core
- Visual: stable red block / foundation motif
- Text:
  - The committed core
  - Still loyal. Still holding the line.
- Purpose: establish remaining strength inside the party

### Scene 3 — The demoralized middle
- Visual: cracked but intact panel
- Text:
  - The demoralized middle
  - Still here. Less sure. Waiting for direction.
- Purpose: show risk of stagnation

### Scene 4 — The drifting fringe
- Visual: fragments dissolving or dispersing
- Text:
  - The drifting fringe
  - Not angry. Just slowly disappearing.
- Purpose: highlight silent attrition

### Scene 5 — The younger reformists
- Visual: rising line / vector / beam
- Text:
  - The younger reformists
  - Energetic. Future-facing. Impatient for renewal.
- Purpose: show latent renewal energy

### Scene 6 — Strategic warning
- Visual: strong centred type, minimal background
- Text:
  - Loyalty is outrunning confidence.
  - If we do not act,
  - drift becomes decline.
- Purpose: drive urgency and discussion

## Optional ending card
- Text: For internal discussion only.
- Use only if needed for context.

## Editing notes
- Hold each scene long enough for easy mobile reading.
- Use gentle motion only: fade, slide, subtle zoom.
- Avoid over-editing. The authority should come from clarity.
