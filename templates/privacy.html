Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters

Last updated: August 10, 2026
Data controller: 琳嫣 Platform
Countries: Global · Privacy Shield compliant

1. Information we collect

We collect data necessary to provide your video generation services securely and reliably.

Account information: When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and a secure password. Your password is hashed using industry-standard algorithms and never stored in plain text.

Content you create: When you use 琳嫣 to generate videos, we process and store:

  • Story text or storyboard files you upload (.md format)
  • Video generation settings (models, aspect ratio, style presets, etc.)
  • Generated video outputs and associated metadata
  • Chat conversations with our AI planning system

Usage data: We automatically collect:

  • IP address and approximate location (country/city level)
  • Browser type, language, and device information
  • Page views, feature usage, and interaction patterns
  • Performance metrics (generation time, error rates)
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies (see Section 5)

Payment information (when available): Currently, our billing system is manual. When we implement automated payments, payment details will be processed through PCI-DSS compliant processors, and we will never store full card numbers.

2. How we use your information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide and improve services: Generate videos from your stories, maintain your workspace, and deliver generated content
  • Authenticate and secure access: Verify your identity, manage sessions, and protect against unauthorized access
  • Personalize your experience: Remember your preferences, recommended models, and usage patterns
  • Operate and improve infrastructure: Monitor system performance, debug issues, and develop new features
  • Comply with legal obligations: Respond to legal requests and protect our rights and safety
  • Communication: Send service announcements, security alerts, and (with consent) product updates
AI processing notice: Your story content and generated videos may be used to improve our AI models, but will be anonymized and aggregated. You retain full ownership of your generated content.

3. Data sharing and disclosures

We do not sell your personal information. We only share data in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers: We use third-party processors for hosting, authentication, payment processing, and AI model infrastructure (including ByteDance models via Flowdaptor). These providers are contractually obligated to protect your data and use it only for provided services.
  • Legal compliance: We may disclose information when required by law, court order, or government request. We will challenge overbroad requests and notify you when legally permitted.
  • Business transfers: In case of merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your data may be transferred as part of the transaction. You will be notified via email or website notice.
  • Protect rights and safety: We may share information to prevent harm, fraud, or illegal activity, or to enforce our terms and policies.
International transfers: Your data may be processed in countries outside your residence. We use Standard Contractual Clauses and other safeguards to ensure adequate protection. Contact us for details at privacy@linyan.ai.

4. Data retention and deletion

Retention periods:

  • Account data: We retain your account information for as long as your account is active or as required by law
  • Generated content: Videos and storyboards are retained for 90 days after generation unless you delete them earlier
  • Usage logs: Aggregated analytics are retained indefinitely; individual usage logs are retained for 12 months
  • Communication records: Support tickets and messages are retained for 24 months

Delete your data: You can:

  • Download all your generated content at any time
  • Request account deletion by contacting support@linyan.ai
  • Permanently delete generated content through your workspace settings

After account deletion, we will remove your personal information from our active databases within 30 days. Some information may be retained in backup systems for legal compliance and will be securely erased within 180 days.

5. Cookies and tracking technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience:

Category Name Purpose Duration
Essential session_id, auth_token Authenticate you and maintain your session Session / 30 days

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Essential cookies cannot be disabled as they are necessary for our services to function.

6. Your rights and choices

Data subject rights: Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion: Request removal of your personal data
  • Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Objection: Opt out of direct marketing and automated decision-making
  • Consent withdrawal: Withdraw consent for data processing (may limit service functionality)

How to exercise your rights:

  • Email: privacy@linyan.ai
  • Processing time: We respond within 30 days
  • Verification: We may request verification of your identity

Do Not Track: Our systems do not respond to browser Do-Not-Track signals. We only track with your consent or as necessary for service operation.

7. Security measures

We implement multiple layers of security:

  • Encryption: All data in transit uses TLS 1.3+ encryption. Data at rest is encrypted using AES-256
  • Access controls: Role-based access to systems; mandatory MFA for administrative accounts
  • Code security: Regular security audits, penetration testing, and code reviews
  • Data handling: Strict confidentiality agreements with all employees and contractors
  • Infrastructure: Cloud infrastructure with SOC 2 Type II compliance

In case of data breach: We will notify affected users and relevant authorities within 72 hours of discovery, as required by applicable law.

8. Children's privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13 (or 16 in EU jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we discover such data, we will delete it promptly. Parents may contact us to request deletion of their child's data.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by:

  • Posting the updated policy on this page
  • Displaying a notice in our platform
  • Sending email notification to registered users
  • Updating the "Last updated" date above

Your continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
